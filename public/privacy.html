MATCHVAGA

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026.

1. Overview

MatchVaga (available at matchvaga.kubezen.com) is a tool that compares a candidate's resume with a job description and generates a compatibility assessment. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is used, and how long it is stored.

2. Data you provide

Resume file: the file (PDF or DOCX) is processed entirely in your browser and is never sent to our servers. Only the extracted text is sent to generate the analysis.

Resume text and job description: sent to our API to generate the assessment. They are stored temporarily on Cloudflare's infrastructure with automatic expiration within 24 hours and are not shared with third parties beyond our processing providers (Cloudflare Workers KV and the artificial intelligence API used for the analysis).

Payment data: payments are processed exclusively by Stripe (stripe.com). We never receive, store, or access your full card details.

3. Data collected automatically

We collect anonymous usage events (pages visited, funnel steps, chosen language) to measure service performance. These events do not personally identify you. We also temporarily record IP addresses for abuse control (per-IP usage limit), without linking them to other data.

Your browser stores locally (localStorage) your language preference and your analysis session state; this data stays on your device.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent data. Data is processed by the following providers, under data protection agreements:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, temporary storage (KV), and abuse protection.
  • AI provider — processing the text to generate the analysis.
  • Stripe — payment processing.

5. Retention

Analysis results are automatically removed within 24 hours. Aggregated funnel events are kept for 31 days. Total counters are kept without personal data.

6. Your rights

You can request deletion of data associated with your usage at any time by contacting the email listed in the footer. Since we do not keep user accounts, deletion is performed based on your analysis session identifier.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: contato@kubezen.com
